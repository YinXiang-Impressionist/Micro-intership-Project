THE ZERO-RISK ENGINE

How MicroIntern SG Works

A clean, mutual-win flywheel built specifically for Singapore's local business ecosystem.

01

SME Posts a 2–4h Task (Free)

Company posts a concrete business bottleneck (e.g. data scraping, onboarding UX audit, social copy). Zero cost. Zero payroll commitment. You do not pay each applicant.

02

Students Submit Real Proof

Multiple students execute and submit structured deliverables. Even if not selected, students gain a verified, real-world case for their portfolio.

03

Review Output & Start a Chat

Company reviews the tangible outputs. If a student's solution stands out, reach out directly for a coffee chat, further project work, or a formal internship offer.
