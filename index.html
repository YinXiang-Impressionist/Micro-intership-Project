THE DAILY-FIT ENGINE

How MicroIntern SG Works

A realistic two-way preview built for Singapore's SMEs and university talent.

01

SME Shares a 2–4h Daily Task (Free)

Company posts a representative sample of its actual daily routine (e.g. data ingestion, customer funnel triage, content drafting). Free to post; zero payroll obligation. You don't pay each applicant.

02

Students Experience the Real Work

Students take on the task, experiencing what working at the company genuinely feels like. Every completed deliverable becomes verified proof in their portfolio.

03

Evaluate Daily Fit & Connect

The employer sees who has the natural instinct and execution skills for their routine operations. Both sides connect for coffee or an internship offer knowing there is a genuine mutual fit.
