THE TIMELINE

The Timeline of a 4-Hour Micro-Internship

Here is how a real task flows from a founder's desk to a signed internship offer, without HR middlemen:

1
SATURDAY 2:00 PM

Claim a Focused 2–4 Hour Challenge

SMEs don't post vague job specs. They post clear, scoped bottlenecks. For example: "Parse 40 Malaysian hardware invoice PDFs into normalized JSON" or "Draft 3 native TikTok hooks for our coffee brand."

2
SATURDAY 5:30 PM

Submit Real Deliverables (Proof of Craft)

No hypothetical cover letters. Students submit concrete deliverables: clean Python scripts with unit tests, structured teardown decks, or short-form video storyboards.

3
SUNDAY 10:00 AM

Founder Review & Direct Offer

The founder opens the file. When they see thoughtful, clean execution, the hiring decision is obvious. The company pays the task honorarium and extends an immediate formal internship invitation.
