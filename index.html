THE REALITY ENGINE

How a Micro-Internship Works

A structured, mutually beneficial workflow designed for Singapore's local business and university ecosystem.

01

SME Posts a Micro-Internship (Free)

The company packages their actual daily workflow into a focused micro-internship (covering business context, execution, and review). Free to post. Zero payroll obligation. You do not pay each applicant.

02

Students Walk Through Real Workflows

Students take on the micro-internship, experiencing the company's real standards and operational rhythms across multiple steps. Every deliverable becomes tangible proof in their portfolio.

03

Evaluate Workflow Fit & Connect

Employers evaluate how candidates perform across the actual routine processes. When there is mutual alignment, both sides connect for a coffee chat or formalize an internship offer.
