A LETTER FROM THE FOUNDER

To the Founder: Are You Sick of ChatGPT-Generated Resumes Yet?

If you run a growing SME in Singapore, you already know the sinking feeling. You post a job opening, and within 48 hours you are flooded with 200 resumes. Every single one is formatted identically, packed with polished buzzwords generated by large language models, and claims they "spearheaded cross-functional synergy."

You spend your precious evenings sifting through them. You shortlist five, interview them, and extend an offer — only to discover on day twenty that they freeze when asked to write a clean database query, troubleshoot a payment drop-off, or draft a coherent customer email without an AI prompt. You just burned two months of intern salary and hours of your team’s focus on a piece of paper.

"You don't hire a chef by reading their essay about culinary theory. You taste their soup. Why are we still hiring knowledge workers by reading their essays?"

To the Student: Tired of Being Rejected for "Lacking Experience"?

And if you are an undergraduate at NUS, NTU, or SMU, you know the other side of this absurd circus. You are hungry to work. You spend your weekends learning real skills, writing code, and analyzing products. But every "entry-level" job portal slams the door in your face: "Requires 2+ years of prior corporate internship experience."

How are you supposed to get your first internship if every company requires you to already have one? You send out 60 cold applications with a template resume, only to be rejected in four seconds by an automated keyword bot. It is insulting, exhausting, and completely disconnected from actual ability.

The Solution: 4 Hours of Real Work, Recognized and Rewarded.

We started MicroIntern SG because both sides deserve better.

Instead of asking students to write resumes, we ask Singapore SMEs to post a self-contained 2–4 hour business bottleneck. Instead of screening applicants with keywords, founders inspect actual deliverables. If a student's output solves your problem, you hire them with 100% conviction. If it doesn't, you keep the report, pay a small honorarium, and lose zero time.

No recruitment agencies. No AI resume arms race. Just proof of work.

THE TIMELINE

How a 4-Hour Micro-Internship Actually Works

Here is how a real task flows from a founder's desk to a signed internship offer, without HR middlemen:

1
SATURDAY 2:00 PM

Claim a Focused 2–4 Hour Challenge

SMEs don't post vague job specs. They post clear, scoped bottlenecks. For example: "Parse 40 Malaysian hardware invoice PDFs into normalized JSON" or "Draft 3 native TikTok hooks for our coffee brand."

2
SATURDAY 5:30 PM

Submit Real Deliverables (Proof of Craft)

No hypothetical cover letters. Students submit concrete deliverables: clean Python scripts with unit tests, structured teardown decks, or short-form video storyboards.

3
SUNDAY 10:00 AM

Founder Review & Direct Offer

The founder opens the file. When they see thoughtful, clean execution, the hiring decision is obvious. The company pays the task honorarium and extends an immediate formal internship invitation.

CASE ARCHIVE

Three Real Work Samples

Inspect how three students without "corporate pedigree" secured direct offers by solving actual Singapore SME problems:

PayBridge SEA

Cross-border payments SME • Tanjong Pagar
3.5 HOURS TASK
THE FOUNDER'S BRIEF:

"Our Singapore-to-Indonesia remittance app loses 42% of business users right at Singpass sync. Analyze 2,000 anonymized session logs and identify where the friction occurs."

STUDENT WORK DELIVERED (DARREN, NUS YEAR 1):

Diagnosis: 68% of drop-offs were due to corporate entity-type mismatch with Singpass MyInfo for Business.

Solution: Designed a fallback 1-click document upload with instant OCR verification.

Feedback from Marcus (COO, PayBridge SEA):

"Darren, your analysis was better than the external agency we paid $5,000 for. We offered him our part-time Product Analyst internship within 48 hours."

OmniSupply AI

Supply chain automation • Jurong Innovation District
4.0 HOURS TASK
THE FOUNDER'S BRIEF:

"We receive erratic invoice PDFs from 40 different Malaysian and Singaporean hardware vendors. Build a resilient Python regex script to parse SKU, quantity, and GST into clean JSON."

STUDENT WORK DELIVERED (SIDDHARTH, NTU YEAR 1):

Architecture: Created a modular rule-based parser with fallback regex scoring. Wrote 15 unit tests covering edge-cases like handwritten GST annotations.

Feedback from Elena (CTO, OmniSupply AI):

"Siddharth caught edge cases we didn't know existed. We sent over an internship contract for next semester immediately."

KopiLabs SG

Heritage specialty coffee brand • Jalan Besar
2.5 HOURS TASK
THE FOUNDER'S BRIEF:

"We need 3 native TikTok script concepts to introduce traditional Nanyang roasting heritage to Gen Z coffee drinkers in Singapore and Malaysia."

STUDENT WORK DELIVERED (CHERYL, SMU YEAR 1):

Hook: 'Why your $8 iced latte is actually lying to your tastebuds (Singapore uncle approved).'

Result: Brand ran test video; hit 340,000 views within 72 hours.

Feedback from Kenji (Founder, KopiLabs SG):

"Cheryl produced 3 killer concepts in an afternoon. She is now heading our content marketing."
