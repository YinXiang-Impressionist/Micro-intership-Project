THE PRACTICAL ENGINE

How a Micro-Internship Works

A realistic two-way evaluation built for Singapore's local business environment.

01

SME Posts a Routine Micro-Internship (Free)

Package a standard routine workflow (e.g. data cleaning, onboarding funnel analysis, or content drafting) into a short micro-internship. Free to post. Zero upfront payroll commitment.

02

Students Walk Through the Real Workflow

Students take on the micro-internship, experiencing the company's actual business rhythm and standards. Completed deliverables become verified work samples in their portfolio.

03

Review Fit & Connect Directly

The employer sees who genuinely fits their day-to-day operations. When there is mutual alignment, both sides connect directly for coffee, a chat, or a formal internship offer.

PRAGMATIC PROOFS

Two Real Micro-Internship Examples

PayBridge SEA

Cross-border payments SME • Tanjong Pagar
Micro-Internship
Routine Workflow Process: Review weekly onboarding event logs, identify friction points for Indonesian business users during Singpass verification, and propose practical low-code improvements.
Student Execution (Darren, NUS Y1): Walked through the complete operational logic: diagnosed corporate entity mismatch errors and delivered clean fallback upload mockups with OCR preview.
Mutual Fit Outcome: Darren experienced real fintech operations; the COO confirmed he could handle routine analytics independently. Hired as a part-time Product Analyst.

OmniSupply AI

Supply chain automation • Jurong Innovation District
Micro-Internship
Routine Workflow Process: Maintain the daily data pipeline: review non-standard supplier invoice formats, build extraction regex scripts, and write unit tests for edge-case variations.
Student Execution (Siddharth, NTU Y1): Completed the end-to-end task according to team coding guidelines, writing clean Python code and 15 tests covering edge-case invoice structures.
Mutual Fit Outcome: Siddharth confirmed he enjoyed the team's engineering stack, while the CTO verified he could contribute to regular sprint cycles from day one. Formal internship offered.
